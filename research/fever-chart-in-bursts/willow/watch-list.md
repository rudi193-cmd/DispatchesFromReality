# Watch list: the four tests

The draft names four tests that tonight sets up. Each has a check-in date.
The check-in dates are proposals, picked to land two weeks, one month and two
months out; move them freely. The convening date is the one fixed point.

## 2026-11-18 check-in: acceptance
Are the results accepted without a rival claim to their authority? This is the
dual sovereignty test asked directly. Look for: concessions, certification
disputes, any claim that a result does not bind.

## 2026-12-04 check-in: the insiders
Do Republicans who distanced themselves before the election close ranks after
it, or does their self-preservation math now point to a coordinated break?
Look for: leadership fights, votes against the administration in the lame duck.

## 2027-01-03 the new Congress convenes
provenance: fitted (press reports give 3 January 2027 as the date the new Congress meets)

## 2027-01-04 check-in: the check
Does the administration treat the new Congress as a legitimate check, or extend
the court-compliance pattern from district courts to the legislature? Look for:
subpoena compliance, appropriations, impoundment.

## 2027-03-01 check-in: delivery
Can the incoming majority produce results people can feel? Brinton's moderates
failed on exactly this. Look for: anything passed and signed, or vetoed, that
touches prices, wages or housing.
