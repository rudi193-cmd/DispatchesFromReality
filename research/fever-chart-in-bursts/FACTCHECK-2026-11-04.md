# Fact-check: The Fever Chart in Bursts (v1)

*Checked 2026-11-04 · named-source claims in [DRAFT.md](DRAFT.md)*

Each claim below was checked against an opened source. The verdicts use this
repo's provenance words: **measured** means read at first hand from the primary
source; **fitted** means corroborated by a secondary report that names the
primary one; **assumed** means not checked.

Every Brinton quotation was matched word for word against the Project Gutenberg
text (eBook 78065, the 1938 Norton first edition). All quoted strings are exact.
Three Brinton paraphrases drift slightly from the text. Of the press claims, one
is wrong as written (the May Senate "refusal"), and two need tighter wording
(the Senate seat count and the Tocqueville line). The checker did not edit
DRAFT.md; every fix in the Wording column, including the five Brinton and
Chenoweth paraphrases and the nominations and voting-record lines, was then
applied to DRAFT.md and README.md in the same change as this note.
